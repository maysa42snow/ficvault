Privacy Policy

Effective & Last Updated: November 19, 2026

At a Glance •

The Short Version

Your fanfiction belongs to you. FicVault uses Firebase Authentication for accounts and Cloud Firestore to sync each user's archive across devices. The archive is private to the signed-in account under the project's security rules:

  • ✦ Account-based cloud sync: Your archive is cached in your browser and synchronized to your private Firebase account space.
  • ✦ Your writing remains yours: FicVault does not claim ownership of your work. Stories are stored in your account's Firestore records so they can sync across devices; authorized project administrators may technically access project data.
  • ✦ Strictly scoped Google Drive sync: If you optionally connect Google Drive, FicVault can only create and update the exact Google Docs it creates—it can never see, read, or modify any other files in your Drive.
01

Overview

FicVault is a web application created to help writers draft, organize, categorize, and archive fanfiction and creative writing. The app runs in your browser, but account sign-in and cross-device archive synchronization use Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. These services process account information and store your archive data in the Firebase project associated with FicVault.

This Privacy Policy explains what is stored in your browser and Firebase account, how the optional Google Drive and Google Docs integration works, and how to request access to or deletion of account data.

02

Information Stored Locally and in Firebase

FicVault keeps a browser copy of your archive in localStorage for responsiveness and offline-friendly editing. The app also synchronizes archive records to Cloud Firestore under your Firebase user ID. Browser storage can include:

  • Story titles, fandoms, and chapters
  • Ship categories and badge colors
  • Tags, summaries, ratings, and status
  • App preferences (theme, view, sort)
  • Google Docs sync settings (folder name, sync status, and synced document IDs)

Important note on browser storage: Clearing browser site data removes that device's local copy, but does not by itself delete your Firebase cloud archive. Keep an independent JSON backup of important writing.

Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore

When you create an account or sign in, Firebase Authentication processes your email address (for email sign-in), a Firebase user identifier, authentication-provider details, and basic profile information such as display name and profile photo URL. FicVault does not receive or store your account password; password handling is provided by Firebase Authentication.

Cloud Firestore stores your profile details, story titles and metadata, chapter content, ships, and archive settings so the same account can load its work on another device. Records are placed under your Firebase user ID. The supplied Firestore Security Rules are intended to allow each signed-in user to read and write only their own records.

Your archive is not intended to be publicly accessible. However, a person with authorized administrator access to the FicVault Firebase project can technically access data stored in that project. The Google Drive integration uses Google Identity Services directly in your browser. FicVault does not receive or store a Google refresh token or Google OAuth client secret. Short-lived Google access tokens are held in browser memory and are discarded when the session ends. Firebase and Google also process data under their own service terms and privacy documentation.

FicVault does not load Firebase Analytics in this implementation. The Analytics measurement ID in the Firebase configuration is not used by the app code.

03

Google Drive & Google Docs Integration

FicVault offers an entirely optional integration with the Google Drive API (v3) so you can back up and edit your stories as native Google Docs in your own Google Drive. A FicVault account is still required to open the writing studio, but you do not need to connect Google Drive to use the archive.

When you choose to connect Google Drive, Google asks you to approve the narrow OAuth 2.0 scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This per-file scope limits the integration to files and folders created or opened by FicVault. The initial authorization is a one-time action; after approval, FicVault can renew access tokens on the server to perform the document updates you request by saving changes in FicVault:

What FicVault CAN Do

  • • Create a dedicated FicVault folder (and optional subfolders organized by Ship) in your Google Drive.
  • • Create native Google Docs for your eligible works and keep them updated automatically after FicVault saves changes.
  • • Update the same Google Docs created by FicVault instead of creating duplicates. Sync is one-way from FicVault to Google Docs.

What FicVault CANNOT Do

  • • View, read, list, search, edit, or delete any other files or folders in your Google Drive.
  • • Access your Gmail, Contacts, Photos, Calendar, or any other Google account services.
  • • Periodically scan your Drive or run scheduled sync jobs. Updates are triggered by saves in FicVault; disconnecting removes the stored authorization.

Browser-based OAuth: FicVault uses Google Identity Services directly in your browser to request the drive.file permission. Google provides a short-lived access token to the browser; FicVault uses that token only to create and update the Google Drive folders and Docs requested by you. FicVault does not send the token to a FicVault server, store a Google refresh token, or store a Google OAuth client secret. A small browser-side authorization marker may be kept locally so FicVault can attempt silent reauthorization on a later visit; Google may still require consent again when the browser session or authorization state has expired.

Automatic sync direction: FicVault sends saved story content and metadata to the Google Docs created for those works. It does not import edits made directly in Google Docs back into the FicVault editor. If the integration detects that a Google Doc was changed outside FicVault after its last successful sync, it pauses automatic updates for that document to reduce the risk of overwriting those changes.

04

Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

FicVault's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In practice, this means the Google API data is used only to create and update the Google Docs and folders requested through FicVault’s automatic save workflow, and for no other purpose. The integration does not use Google API data for advertising, profiling, or AI training.

05

Data Sharing, Tracking & AI Policy

FicVault is built for writers, not data brokers. We uphold the following strict commitments:

  • — No selling or sharing of data: We never sell, rent, trade, or transfer your data or writing to third parties.
  • — No ads or tracking cookies: FicVault contains zero advertising networks, zero cross-site tracking cookies, and zero third-party behavioral analytics.
  • — No AI or machine learning training: Your creative writing, metadata, and any data accessed via Google APIs are never used to train, fine-tune, or evaluate artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
06

Data Retention, Export & Deletion

FicVault stores your archive in Firebase and keeps a browser cache. You can export your writing at any time, disconnect Google Drive, and request account-data deletion through the support contact:

Export Your Work Anytime

You can export individual stories at any time as AO3-ready HTML, Word / Google Docs (.doc), or Markdown (.md), or download your entire archive (stories, chapters, ships, and tags) as a portable JSON backup.

Delete Local Stories or Wipe App Data

Delete stories from FicVault where the app provides that action, or clear this site's data in your browser settings to remove the local cache. Clearing browser data does not delete your Firebase archive or Google Docs.

Disconnect Google Drive

Disconnecting removes FicVault's encrypted refresh-token record and asks Google to revoke the grant. Google Docs already created in your Drive are not deleted; manage those files in Google Drive.

Revoke Google Drive Access

Click Disconnect inside FicVault's Drive Settings at any time, or permanently revoke FicVault's permissions directly from your Google Account Third-Party Permissions page. Revoking access will not delete any Google Docs you have already saved in your Drive.

Request Account or Cloud Archive Deletion

The current settings page does not yet provide a complete account-deletion workflow. To request deletion of your Firebase account and remaining cloud records, contact the support address in the Contact section and include the email address used for your FicVault account. Do not send your password.

Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore process account and archive data as described in this Policy.
