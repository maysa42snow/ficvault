Description of Service

FicVault is a browser-based writing studio and archive organizer built for fanfiction and creative writing. It enables you to draft multi-chapter stories, categorize works by ships, attach tags and summaries, export formatted files, synchronize your archive to a Firebase account, and optionally sync drafts to your own Google Drive as native Google Docs.

Firebase Account & Cloud Archive

FicVault requires an account. Firebase Authentication manages sign-in, and Cloud Firestore stores your profile and archive records under your Firebase user ID. Your browser also keeps a local cache (localStorage) for responsive editing. The supplied Firestore Security Rules restrict each user to their own records. Google Drive sync is a separate, optional integration.

Local Storage, Backups & Data Loss

FicVault automatically keeps a browser copy of your drafts in localStorage and synchronizes your archive to your Firebase account when a connection is available. Browser storage is temporary by nature and can be cleared without warning; cloud access also depends on Firebase service availability and correct project configuration.

Important Backup Responsibility

Local drafts stored in your browser may be permanently lost due to:

  • Clearing browser history, cookies, cache, or site data.
  • Using Private / Incognito browsing windows (which erase storage upon closing).
  • Major browser updates, device resets, storage quota limits, or switching browsers/devices.

You are responsible for keeping independent backups of important work. FicVault provides a full-library JSON Backup export, individual story exports (AO3 HTML, .doc, and Markdown .md), Firebase cloud archive sync, and optional Google Drive sync. Firebase cloud sync is not a substitute for an independent backup.

05

Google Drive & Google Docs Integration

FicVault offers an optional Google Drive and Google Docs integration via Google OAuth 2.0 and the Google Drive API (v3). You can use FicVault without connecting Drive. When you connect it once, FicVault can automatically create and update Docs for your works after changes are saved in the editor.

Scope & Third-Party Terms

When you connect Google Drive, FicVault requests the per-file scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, which limits this integration to files and folders created or opened by FicVault. Google authorization is approved once; a server-side token lets FicVault renew access when an automatic document update is needed. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Automatic sync is one-way: FicVault sends saved editor content to its Google Docs. Changes made directly in Google Docs are not imported into FicVault. If a Doc's modified time changes outside FicVault, automatic updates for that Doc are paused to protect external edits; you may explicitly choose to replace it with the current FicVault version after reviewing the warning.

Disconnecting Drive removes FicVault's stored authorization and requests revocation from Google. Existing files in your Drive are not deleted.

Your use of Google Drive and Google Docs through FicVault is also subject to the Google Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

  • — Disconnect Anytime: You may disconnect Google Drive from inside FicVault's Drive Settings at any time, or permanently revoke access directly through your Google Account Permissions page.
  • — Independent Project: FicVault is an independent creative tool and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Google LLC. Google Drive and Google Docs are trademarks of Google LLC.
