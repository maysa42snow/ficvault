Privacy Policy

Effective & Last Updated: November 19, 2026

At a Glance •

The Short Version

Your fanfiction belongs strictly to you. FicVault is designed from the ground up to keep your writing private without requiring an account on our servers:

  • ✦ 100% browser-based: FicVault runs entirely in your web browser with no backend server and no remote database.
  • ✦ We never collect or read your stories: Your drafts, ship categories, tags, and summaries never leave your device unless you explicitly choose to sync them to your own Google Drive.
  • ✦ Strictly scoped Google Drive sync: If you optionally connect Google Drive, FicVault can only create and update the exact Google Docs it creates—it can never see, read, or modify any other files in your Drive.
01

Overview

FicVault is a free, client-side web application created to help writers draft, organize, categorize, and archive fanfiction and creative writing. Because FicVault operates entirely inside your local web browser, the developer does not operate a backend server or central database and does not collect, view, monitor, or store your stories, account credentials, or personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how your writing and preferences are stored on your own device, how the optional Google Drive and Google Docs integration works, and how you maintain full control over your data at all times.

02

Information Stored Locally

To make sure your work is saved automatically as you write, FicVault stores your archive data directly in your browser's built-in localStorage on your own device. This local data includes:

  • Story titles, fandoms, and chapters
  • Ship categories and badge colors
  • Tags, summaries, ratings, and status
  • App preferences (theme, view, sort)
  • Optional Google Drive sync settings (folder name and synced document IDs)

Important note on browser storage: Data saved in localStorage stays only on the browser and device you use. Clearing your browser's site data or cache will permanently erase your local copies unless you have exported a backup or synced your works to Google Drive.

03

Google Drive & Google Docs Integration

FicVault offers an entirely optional integration with the Google Drive API (v3) so you can back up and edit your stories as native Google Docs in your own Google Drive. You can use FicVault offline or locally without ever signing in to Google.

When you choose to connect Google Drive, FicVault requests only the narrow, non-sensitive OAuth 2.0 scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This per-file scope strictly limits what FicVault can access:

What FicVault CAN Do

  • • Create a dedicated FicVault folder (and optional subfolders organized by Ship) in your Google Drive.
  • • Create new native Google Docs files from the drafts you choose to sync.
  • • Update existing Google Docs that were previously created by FicVault so your edits stay synced without creating duplicates.

What FicVault CANNOT Do

  • • View, read, list, search, edit, or delete any other files or folders in your Google Drive.
  • • Access your Gmail, Contacts, Photos, Calendar, or any other Google account services.
  • • Access your Google Drive in the background after you close the browser tab or disconnect.

Direct Browser-to-Google Authentication: OAuth 2.0 sign-in happens directly between your web browser and Google via Google Identity Services. Your temporary OAuth access token is held strictly in browser memory for your active session—it is never logged, stored on a remote server, or transmitted to the developer or any third party.

04

Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

FicVault's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In practice, this means any data exchanged with Google APIs is used solely to provide the user-facing document syncing features you explicitly trigger inside FicVault, and for no other purpose.

05

Data Sharing, Tracking & AI Policy

FicVault is built for writers, not data brokers. We uphold the following strict commitments:

  • — No selling or sharing of data: We never sell, rent, trade, or transfer your data or writing to third parties.
  • — No ads or tracking cookies: FicVault contains zero advertising networks, zero cross-site tracking cookies, and zero third-party behavioral analytics.
  • — No AI or machine learning training: Your creative writing, metadata, and any data accessed via Google APIs are never used to train, fine-tune, or evaluate artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
06

Data Retention, Export & Deletion

Because FicVault stores nothing on external servers, you retain 100% control over keeping, exporting, or deleting your data at any time:

Export Your Work Anytime

You can export individual stories at any time as AO3-ready HTML, Word / Google Docs (.doc), or Markdown (.md), or download your entire archive (stories, chapters, ships, and tags) as a portable JSON backup.

Delete Local Stories or Wipe App Data

Delete any story, chapter, or ship directly inside FicVault, or clear this site's data in your browser settings to immediately remove all locally stored information.

Revoke Google Drive Access

Click Disconnect inside FicVault's Drive Settings at any time, or permanently revoke FicVault's permissions directly from your Google Account Third-Party Permissions page. Revoking access will not delete any Google Docs you have already saved in your Drive.

07

Children's Privacy

FicVault does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the minimum digital consent age applicable in your jurisdiction). Because FicVault does not operate user accounts or collect personal data on any server, no personal information from minors is transmitted to or stored by the developer.
