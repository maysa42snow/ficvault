Connect once, then FicVault updates your Docs automatically.

Checking Google Drive…

Your Google authorization is linked to this FicVault account.

One-time permission: FicVault suggests the email on your FicVault account, and Google asks you to approve access to create and update only the files and folders it creates. You can choose a different Google account if needed; FicVault does not get access to your entire Drive.

Automatic, one-way sync: edits made in FicVault are pushed to Google Docs. Edits made directly in Google Docs are not imported into FicVault; if FicVault detects an outside Doc edit, it pauses that document to protect your changes.

You can disconnect Google Drive at any time. Your existing Docs remain in your Drive.

Google Docs sync is separate from your automatic FicVault cloud archive sync.

Library Backup & Restore
